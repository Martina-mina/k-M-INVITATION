WEDDING INVITATION WEBSITE
============================

1) Open index.html in Chrome/Edge.
2) To change names/date/text, edit index.html.
3) To change photos:
   - Put your JPG/PNG images inside assets/
   - Change the matching src="assets/...." in index.html.
4) Countdown date is near the bottom of index.html:
   const weddingDate = new Date("December 18, 2026 20:00:00").getTime();
5) Change the Google Maps and WhatsApp links in index.html.

The envelope opening animation, responsive design, gallery lightbox,
countdown, sections and styling are already included.
